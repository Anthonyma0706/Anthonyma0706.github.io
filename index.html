About

I work at the intersection of AI, computational biology, and education. From McGill to Harvard to MIT, my research has focused on applying machine learning to complex biological and social systems.

At Harvard Medical School, I worked with Prof. Faisal Mahmood on multimodal representation learning for computational pathology. At MIT, I collaborated with Prof. Manolis Kellis at CSAIL and the Broad Institute.

My master's research explored Machine Learning for Suggesting Personalized Intervention for Obesity — bridging data science with real-world health outcomes.

Currently, I'm co-founder of LeadLearning, applying AI and systems thinking to education — building tools that transform how students learn.
