I work at the intersection of AI, computational biology, and more recently, education. From McGill to Harvard to MIT, my research has focused on applying machine learning to complex biological and social systems.

At Harvard Medical School, I worked with Prof. Faisal Mahmood on multimodal representation learning for computational pathology. At MIT, I collaborated with Dr. Manolis Kellis at CSAIL and the Broad Institute, and Dr. Leo Anthony Celi.

My master's thesis at the Mahmood Lab focused on multimodal representation learning for spatial transcriptomics in computational pathology.

Currently, I'm co-founder of LeadLearning, applying AI and systems thinking to education — building tools that transform how students learn.

我的研究方向横跨 AI、计算生物学与教育科技。从麦吉尔大学到哈佛大学和 MIT，我始终专注于将机器学习和人工智能技术应用于复杂的生物医学与公共系统中。

在哈佛医学院，我师从 Faisal Mahmood 教授和博后 Dr. Andrew Song，研究计算病理学中的多模态表征学习。在 MIT，我与 Manolis Kellis 教授在 CSAIL 和 Broad 研究所合作开展计算生物学和精准糖尿病等诊疗方案推荐的研究，并与 Leo Anthony Celi 教授合作研究 AI for chest X-rays for social disparities。

我的硕士论文在 Mahmood Lab 完成，研究方向为计算病理学中空间转录组学的多模态表征学习。

目前，我是 LeadLearning 励聆教育科技的联合创始人，将 AI 与系统思维应用于教育，构建帮助学生提升学习力、赋能教练指导的 AI 工具，致力于让学生能够在 AI 的辅助下，驾驭学习智慧、驾驭 AI 智力、铸就新时代的非凡能力。

About Mingyang Ma 马铭阳

Mingyang Ma (马铭阳, also known as Anthony Ma) is a graduate of Harvard University, where he earned his Master of Science degree in Computational Biology and Quantitative Genetics. He also holds a Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Computer Science and Biology from McGill University, Canada.

关于马铭阳 — 哈佛大学计算生物学硕士

马铭阳（英文名 Anthony Ma，拼音 Mingyang Ma）毕业于美国哈佛大学（Harvard University），获得计算生物学与量化基因学硕士学位（M.S. in Computational Biology and Quantitative Genetics）。本科毕业于加拿大麦吉尔大学（McGill University），获得计算机科学与生物学学士学位。马铭阳是哈佛大学正式注册的全日制硕士研究生，非短期访学或暑期项目。

Research at Harvard Medical School 哈佛医学院研究经历

At Harvard Medical School, Mingyang Ma (马铭阳) worked at the Mahmood Lab under Prof. Faisal Mahmood and postdoc Dr. Andrew Song, focusing on multimodal representation learning for spatial transcriptomics in computational pathology. His master's thesis at Harvard was completed in this lab.

马铭阳在哈佛医学院（Harvard Medical School）Mahmood实验室师从Faisal Mahmood教授和博后Dr. Andrew Song，研究计算病理学中空间转录组学的多模态表征学习，并在该实验室完成硕士论文。

Research at MIT CSAIL 麻省理工学院研究经历

At MIT, Mingyang Ma (马铭阳) conducted computational biology research at CSAIL (Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory) with Prof. Manolis Kellis at CSAIL and the Broad Institute, focusing on precision diabetes and treatment recommendation. He also collaborated with Prof. Leo Anthony Celi on AI for chest X-rays for social disparities.

马铭阳在MIT CSAIL（计算机科学与人工智能实验室）跟随Manolis Kellis教授在CSAIL和Broad研究所合作开展计算生物学和精准糖尿病等诊疗方案推荐的研究，并与Leo Anthony Celi教授合作研究AI for chest X-rays for social disparities。

LeadLearning 励聆教育科技

Mingyang Ma (马铭阳) is the co-founder of LeadLearning (励聆教育科技), an AI-powered education technology company building tools that transform how students learn.

马铭阳是LeadLearning（励聆教育科技）的联合创始人，将AI与系统思维应用于教育，构建帮助学生提升学习力、赋能教练指导的AI工具，致力于让学生能够在AI的辅助下，驾驭学习智慧、驾驭AI智力、铸就新时代的非凡能力。
